The sky model simobserve observes (simulation Phase 5b, #841): CASA 6.7.6’s simutil.modifymodel and simutil.flatimage, ia.modify adding a component list to an image, and the component-only model simobserve makes when it has no image.

The images written are what CASA writes: their shape, brightness unit, coordinate values, Stokes and spectral axes, and pixels. Their coordinate systems carry CASA’s ia.fromshape observation information (ALMA, “Karl Jansky”, 2000-01-01), and the spectral axis is LSRK with no conversion layer. The tests compare a grid of CASA’s outputs (tests/data/skymodel_casa.json.gz, from tools/simulation/skymodel_oracle.py): strings exactly, coordinate values to 1e-12, pixels to 1e-6 of the peak.

Where casa-rs differs, each recorded in docs/CASA (C++) bugs.md:

  • Axis order. modifymodel reorders the axes by swapping each into place, which leaves a cyclic order (spectral axis first) in the wrong order, and CASA then raises “internal error: I’m confused about the shape if your model data cube”; casa-rs permutes the axes.
  • Stokes labels. CASA’s new image takes its Stokes axis from ia.fromshape, which labels planes by their count (I, IQ, IQU, IQUV): an I,V model comes out labelled I,Q, and three unlabelled planes come out IQU though modifymodel reports and warns IQV. casa-rs keeps the model’s labels, and labels unlabelled planes as it reports them.
  • A cell per axis. modifymodel takes incell as a list of the two axes’ cells, but under Python 3 indexes the map it makes of them and raises TypeError; casa-rs takes the two cells, as CASA’s Python 2 code did.
  • An unset compwidth. The component-only model’s spectral increment is compwidth as given; with none, CASA’s coordsys.setincrement puts a meaningless value there (the model’s right ascension, in radians, as hertz). casa-rs uses the 2 GHz simobserve observes with.
  • Frequency frames without a usable frame. A component whose spectrum is in another frame than the image’s is converted into the image’s frame with the image’s conversion-layer frame, as CASA converts it. When the image has none, or the placeholder ia.fromshape leaves (an epoch of 0 at the geocentre), CASA converts through the placeholder; casa-rs leaves the frequencies unconverted (add_components).
