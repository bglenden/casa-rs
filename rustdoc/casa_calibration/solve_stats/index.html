How far a calibration solve’s intervals got, as CASA’s gaincal and bandpass return it in solvestats (#931).

CASA 6.7.6.14 counts solution intervals, not solutions or channels, at four stages, one count per solved polarization: the intervals there are (expected), those with unflagged data (data_unflagged), those that keep minblperant baselines (above_minblperant) and those whose solution passes minsnr (above_minsnr). It keeps the counts for each spectral window, for each antenna in it, and summed over the windows, and counts each antenna’s intervals as the reference antenna (used_as_refant). What CASA does, established by running it:

  • every window and antenna of the MeasurementSet has counts, zero where the selection does not reach it;
  • an antenna’s expected intervals are those its selected cross-correlation rows reach, flagged or not; a window’s are all its intervals;
  • an antenna has unflagged data in a polarization when a baseline of it has positive unflagged weight there, after a flag on any correlation has flagged the sample for all of them;
  • a window’s above_minsnr counts the intervals that pass minblperant, whatever their solutions’ SNR;
  • above_minsnr counts the solutions minsnr left, before the reference antenna is chosen;
  • an antenna is used as the reference antenna in an interval whose gains are finally referred to it, with the reference antenna flex chooses where the preferred one is flagged, and that kept its solution.
