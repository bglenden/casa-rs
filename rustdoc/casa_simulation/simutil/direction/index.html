Module direction 

Source
Expand description

Directions as simutil writes them: 'J2000 18:00:00.03100 -022.59.59.600000', an epoch, a longitude in qa.formxxx’s hms and a latitude in its dms, both at precision 5.

Functions§

average_direction
simutil.average_direction(directions): the running mean of the longitudes (each wrapped to within 180° of the mean so far) and of the latitudes, with the first direction’s epoch. A direction in another epoch is averaged as it is (CASA warns that precession is not handled).
direction_measure
simutil.dir_s2m(direction) of a direction string: the measure me.direction makes of its last two fields, read as degrees when they have no unit, in the frame its first field names (J2000 when it has two fields, or names none casa-types knows). The angles are as the measure holds them, the longitude in (−π, π] (MVDirection::get).
format_direction
simutil.dir_m2s: a direction measure’s reference code and its longitude and latitude as simutil writes them.
is_direction
simutil.isdirection(text): whether text splits as a direction. casatools’ me.direction accepts any reference code it is then given (an unknown one falls back to J2000), so splitting is the whole test.
median_direction
simutil.median_direction(directions): NumPy’s median of the longitudes and of the latitudes. As in CASA, the longitudes are first wrapped to within 180° of 0°, not of each other.
split_direction
simutil.direction_splitter(direction): the epoch and the two angles of a direction string, in degrees.
wrap_angle
simutil.wrapang(ang, target, period): angle moved by whole periods to within half a period of target.
