Module baseline 

Source
Expand description

Interferometer baselines to uvw coordinates, as casacore computes them.

UvwFrame is casacore’s MBaseline conversion from ITRF to J2000 followed by the MVuvw projection on a J2000 phase centre: the chain NewMSSimulator::calcAntUVW and MSUVWGenerator use to give each antenna its uvw. The conversion routes the baseline direction through HADEC, TOPO, APP and JNAT, applying Earth rotation (local apparent sidereal time), polar motion, diurnal aberration, precession and nutation (IAU 1976/1980), annual aberration and solar light deflection. The baseline’s length is unchanged.

An antenna’s uvw is that of the baseline from the observatory to the antenna (observatory minus antenna, casacore’s MVBaseline(first, second) convention). A row’s uvw is the second antenna’s minus the first’s, so the observatory term cancels.

The epoch is the caller’s. casacore’s simulator reads a row’s stored time as UT1; this module converts whatever epoch it is given.
