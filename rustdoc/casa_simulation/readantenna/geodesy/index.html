Module geodesy 

Source
Expand description

The geodesy of CASA’s simutil: its datum and ellipsoid tables and its conversions between UTM, geodetic and Earth-centred coordinates.

CASA (simutil)casa-rs
getdatum(datumcode)datum, DATUMS, ELLIPSOIDS
utm2long(east, north, zone, datum, nors)utm_to_geodetic
long2xyz(lon, lat, elevation, datum)geodetic_to_xyz
utm2xyz(easting, northing, elevation, zone, datum, nors)utm_to_xyz
xyz2long(x, y, z, datum)xyz_to_geodetic
locxyz2itrf(lat, longitude, alt, locx, locy, locz)local_to_itrf

The arithmetic follows CASA’s Python operation by operation (including its 180/π divisions and its ** powers), so results agree with CASA’s to rounding. As in CASA, the “ITRF” these functions produce is the nominal right-handed Earth-centred frame of the datum’s ellipsoid shifted by the datum’s offsets, not a realised ITRF.

The UTM conversion is T. Vincenty’s transverse Mercator inversion (subroutines tconpc and tmgeod of the NGS UTMS program, 1985), which R. Indebetouw translated from Fortran for CASA in 2009.

Structs§

Datum
A geodetic datum of CASA’s getdatum table: an ellipsoid and the offsets that take the datum’s Earth-centred coordinates to WGS 84’s.
Ellipsoid
A reference ellipsoid of CASA’s getdatum table.

Constants§

DATUMS
CASA’s datum table (getdatum’s datums), in CASA’s order.
ELLIPSOIDS
CASA’s ellipsoid table (getdatum’s ellipsoids), in CASA’s order.
WGS84
The WGS 84 datum, which xyz2long uses for an unknown observatory.

Functions§

datum
Look a datum up by its CASA code (getdatum).
geodetic_to_xyz
Convert geodetic longitude, latitude and elevation on a datum to Earth-centred x, y, z (CASA’s long2xyz).
local_to_itrf
Convert local tangent-plane offsets to Earth-centred x, y, z (CASA’s locxyz2itrf, from Rob Reid).
utm_to_geodetic
Convert UTM coordinates to geodetic longitude and latitude (CASA’s utm2long).
utm_to_xyz
Convert UTM easting, northing and elevation to Earth-centred x, y, z (CASA’s utm2xyz): utm_to_geodetic then geodetic_to_xyz.
xyz_to_geodetic
Convert Earth-centred x, y, z to geodetic longitude, latitude and height on a datum (CASA’s xyz2long, a transcription of SOFA’s gc2gde).
