pub fn bind_to( &self, other: &Self, tolerance: &BindingTolerance, ) -> Result<Binding, ObservationError>

Bind this catalogue’s entities to other’s by content: antennas by station and position within tolerance, fields by phase centre (frame and angles within tolerance), scans by casacore number (by name when a scan has none), observing sessions by ObservationEntry::same_session, and partitions by PartitionEntry::same_content together with the bound session, the bound sets of fields and of scans (scans when both stores name them) and the feed pair (when both stores have one). Names and list positions decide nothing: converters rename antennas and fields.

The binding is one to one: a key one store lacks (MSv4 has no feed axis; an MSv2 may hold no archive identifiers) does not distinguish, but two entities this catalogue keeps apart never bind to one entity of other.

§Errors

When an entity of this catalogue matches none of other’s, or more than one, or two of them match the same one: an ambiguity is refused, never resolved by position.
