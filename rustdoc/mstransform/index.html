mstransform - tutorial-scoped MeasurementSet transform, the mstransform and split task provider, on the shared task front end. Its first word names the surface (mstransform split ...); mstransform runs otherwise.

The channels averaged into each output channel: split’s width, or mstransform’s chanbin when its chanaverage is set and 1 otherwise, as CASA’s tasks take them. CASA’s mstransform refuses to average by one channel; split’s width=1 averages nothing.
