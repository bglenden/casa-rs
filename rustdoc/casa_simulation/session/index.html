The simulator session: CASA’s sm tool in Rust.

A Simulator holds what the sm setters describe, as CASA’s Simulator and NewMSSimulator would have written it to the subtables, and the scans observe plans. It writes nothing until Simulator::close, which creates the MeasurementSet in one streamed pass (plan §4, deferred single-pass execution), so no reader can see an intermediate state.

The setters keep CASA’s semantics, including order-dependent ones: setfield records the reference time and first spectral window in force when it is called, setconfig and setfeed append, and observe reads the limits, autocorrelation weight and antennas defined at that moment. Where CASA crashes, writes garbage or corrupts data, the session refuses or diverges, as recorded in docs/CASA (C++) bugs.md.
