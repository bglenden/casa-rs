What a source holds that a conversion cannot carry, found before anything is written.

The inventory is of whole tables and columns: an MSv2’s SIGMA, FLAG_ROW flags, FLAG_CATEGORY cells, ARRAY_IDs other than 0, FEED receptors that vary in time (of which the catalogue keeps the last row, as casacore’s MSIter does), MAIN columns outside the boundary’s roles and subtables the catalogue does not carry (system calibration, weather, flag commands, history, Doppler, frequency offsets, and any further subtable a keyword names), and into an MSv4, which holds less of them than the catalogue, its SOURCE rows, PROCESSOR_IDs other than 0, a PROCESSOR of more than one row and STATE rows that say more than their intents; an MSv4’s sub-datasets and main-dataset variables the writer does not write; and in either the data groups not selected; and, into an MSv4, antennas no partition’s baselines name. What an MSv2 destination’s subtables write as MSv2’s default is found by writing them (super::msv2_tables::rehearse). What pointing carries is decided by super::pointing_plan. Columns of the subtables the catalogue does carry (an antenna’s offset, a field’s delay direction, a spectral window’s resolution) are not inventoried, but an ephemeris table’s are (ephemeris_tables), since the catalogue carries only the body’s directions, distances and velocities. Fields whose direction is a polynomial in time are refused: the catalogue would hold a constant term as the phase centre.
