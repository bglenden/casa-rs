Module refractivity 

Source
Expand description

Complex refractivity of the atmospheric gases (ATM’s RefractiveIndex).

For each absorbing species ATM sums Van Vleck–Weisskopf line profiles over its line catalogue,

F(ν) = (ν/νₗ) [ (1 − iδ)/(νₗ − ν − iΔν) − (1 + iδ)/(νₗ + ν + iΔν) ]

with pressure (and, where it matters, Doppler) broadening Δν and, for O2, line interference δ. Each line is weighted by its intensity and Boltzmann factor, and the sum by the dipole moment and partition function. The result is the species’ specific refractivity: a complex number whose imaginary part is the absorption cross-section (m²) and whose real part is the phase delay cross-section (rad·m²). Multiplying by a number density gives the absorption (m⁻¹) and phase (rad/m) per unit path; ATM’s layer refractivities are those products (o2_refractivity, h2o_refractivity, …).

The water and dry continua (collision-induced absorption) are empirical and already per unit path.

Frequencies are in GHz, temperatures in kelvin and pressures in hPa, as in ATM. ATM restricts each line sum to the lines within a few GHz of the frequency, through window tables indexed by 2-GHz frequency bins; the port keeps those windows, so it sums exactly the lines ATM sums.

Functions§

density_refractivity
A species’ refractivity per unit path for a number density in molecules/m³, (rad/m, 1/m): ATM’s getRefractivity_16o16o16o and its siblings for the ozone species, and its getEarthSpecificRefractivity_* times the density for the minor gases. These species take no water vapour pressure.
h2o_refractivity
A water line species’ refractivity per unit path, (rad/m, 1/m): the specific refractivity times the water number density for the partial pressure water_vapour_pressure_hpa (ATM’s getRefractivity_hh16o and its siblings).
o2_refractivity
The O2 lines’ refractivity per unit path, (rad/m, 1/m): the specific refractivity times the O2 number density at pressure_hpa (ATM’s getRefractivity_16o16o and its isotopologue siblings).
specific_refractivity
The specific refractivity of species (ATM’s mkEarthSpecificRefractivity): the line species’ cross-sections in (rad·m², m²) per molecule of the parent gas, or the continua’s refractivity per unit path in (rad/m, 1/m)… as ATM defines them.
