The device requested resolves to for problem at precision on host. cpu and gpu are taken as asked (check refuses a gpu request the device cannot run). auto takes the GPU where check admits the problem there and the GPU route has been compared with the CPU’s end to end (gpu_checked), and the CPU otherwise: off macOS, without a device, for the W and AW kernel sets, for an explicit gridprecision = f64, and for outlier fields, polarizations other than Stokes I and visibility writes. A mosaic goes to the GPU since its Metal laws landed (#787; the owner kept auto’s rule, 2026-10-19).
