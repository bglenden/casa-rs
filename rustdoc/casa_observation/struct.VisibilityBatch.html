Expand description

Whole times of one partition’s time × baseline grid, their coordinates and one data group’s payload, borrowed from the caller’s BatchBuffers and the observation’s Catalogue.

Sample t × B + b is time t of the batch and baseline b of the partition’s table (Self::baselines); the coordinates cost T + B entries, not T × B. Both stores deliver this shape: MSv4 holds it, the MSv2 adapter places rows on it at opening and pads the cells no row filled (flag true, values and weights NaN). Field and scan are per time.

Every view is filled and checked before the batch is delivered: reading an element performs no I/O or decoding. The borrow keeps the buffers from being refilled while any view of them is alive.

Fields§

§catalogue: &'a Arc<Catalogue>

The observation’s catalogue, which the batch was checked against: every identity in the batch (partition, field, scan, antenna) names an entry of it.

§partition: PartitionId

The partition the samples belong to.

§time: EpochView<'a>

The batch’s times, in the partition’s scale and origin.

§baselines: &'a [OrderedBaseline]

The partition’s baseline table, from the catalogue.

§field: ArrayView1<'a, FieldId>

The field at each time.

§scan: Option<ArrayView1<'a, ScanId>>

The scan at each time, when requested.

§timing: SampleTiming<'a>

The samples’ time auxiliaries.

§first_channel: usize

The partition’s native channel of this batch’s first channel.

§frequency: FrequencyView<'a>

The channels’ frequencies: a window of the catalogue’s.

§correlations: &'a [Correlation]

The correlation products along the last axis: the delivered ones, in the partition’s order.

§group: DataGroupView<'a>

The requested data group.

§fragments: &'a [GroupFragment]

The scientific-group fragments in this batch, in sample order. Only a delivered batch has them; its compute windows have none.

pub fn channel_window( &self, range: Range<usize>, ) -> Result<Self, ObservationError>

A compute window of channels range (relative to this batch’s channels) of every sample: views of the same buffers, nothing copied. The window carries no fragments: group completion stays with the delivered batch, so splitting a batch among work units cannot announce a group’s end more than once.

§Errors

If range is outside the batch’s channels.
