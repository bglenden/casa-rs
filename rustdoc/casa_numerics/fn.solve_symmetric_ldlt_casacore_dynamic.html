Solve a dynamically sized symmetric system with casacore LSQFit ordering.

normal is a row-major square matrix whose upper triangle contains the symmetric system. This is the arbitrary-order counterpart of solve_symmetric_ldlt_casacore for polynomial reconstruction bases.
