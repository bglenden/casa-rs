CASA-compatible MeasurementSet simulation.

This crate is the Rust side of CASA’s simulator tool (sm) and of the simobserve, simanalyze and simalma tasks (docs/simulation/simulation-plan-20261020.md, #841). Prediction goes through the imaging measurement operator, so simulation and imaging share one forward model.

simutil.readantenna(antab)readantenna::read_antenna_config

CASA’s sm has no execution controls; a session’s memory budget, workers and backend (ExecutionControls, SessionResources) are the Python tool’s constructor arguments. close predicts a model on the CPU or the host’s Metal device (on the CPU only through WProjectFT) and draws noise on the CPU. Where the session departs from CASA is recorded in docs/CASA (C++) bugs.md.
