Expand description

An opened MSv4 processing set (XRADIO VisibilityXds schema 4.0.1 on local Zarr v3) and its catalogue.

Each visibility dataset is one partition, described by its content (spectral window, correlations, the intents of its scan_name, its observation_info). Antennas are the union of the datasets’ antenna_xds entries, one per name and station (one with two positions is refused), in an order that keeps every dataset’s: a dataset lists only its baselines’ antennas, in its MSv2’s ANTENNA order when either converter wrote it, so the order the datasets determine is that MSv2’s. Fields come from each data group’s own field_and_source dataset and are distinct unless name, phase centre and source all agree: two groups with different phase centres keep their own fields and UVW frames, and a traversal of either delivers its own. A field_and_source_ephemeris dataset’s fields follow its ephemeris, in either of XRADIO’s layouts (msv4/ephemeris.rs), and are one field across datasets when their offsets agree and their samples agree where both hold them: the field then follows the samples of both. An ephemeris that ends before the TDB of a dataset’s last time, as XRADIO’s can, gains one sample extrapolated to it (casa_observation::FieldEphemeris::extrapolated). Opening checks every variable’s data type and dimensions, and every unit and frame, against the profile and refuses others rather than reinterpreting or rescaling them.

pub fn open_granted( path: impl AsRef<Path>, options: &OpenOptions, grant: &mut dyn FnMut(usize) -> Result<(), ObservationError>, ) -> Result<Self, ObservationError>

Self::open_with, asking grant for the bytes the opening will hold: metadata before it is parsed, then before decoding any dataset what each dataset holds once open and, beyond, the largest dataset’s decoding, projected from the arrays’ shapes and element sizes (the opening module). The processing set’s metadata documents and the projection are read first. An error from grant refuses the opening with nothing more decoded. What the opened observation holds is Self::held_bytes, to which a caller that admitted the grant trims or raises its charge.

§Errors

As Self::open, and whatever grant returns.
