Module shadowing 

Source
Expand description

Antennas shadowed by their neighbours, from projected baselines.

Two dishes whose apertures overlap as seen from the source block each other’s view: the one behind, farther from the source, is shadowed. The blocked fraction of each aperture follows casacore’s NewMSSimulator::blockage, from the projected separation √(u²+v²) and the two diameters; an antenna is shadowed when the fraction exceeds a limit.

With casacore’s uvw convention, a row’s uvw is R·(X₁ − X₂), so w > 0 puts antenna 1 nearer the source and antenna 2 behind it. CASA’s simulator (NewMSSimulator.cc 1812-1815) assumes the opposite sign and charges the blockage to the antenna in front. CASA’s own flagger (FlagAgentShadow, since casapy 4.1) charges it to the one behind. ShadowedAntenna chooses between them.
