pub fn optimum_image_size(pixels: usize) -> usize
Expand description

The image side CASA calls efficient for pixels: the smallest even size from pixels (rounded up to even) whose prime factors are all at most 5, searched up to the size made by raising each of the even size’s prime factors above 5 to the next number with no such factor.

tclean warns when a side is not its own optimum (SynthesisParamsImage::verify), and simutil.imtclean replaces it. This is SynthesisUtilMethods::getOptimumSize with its primeFactors, as CASA 6.7.6 computes them: 0 stays 0 and 1 becomes 2.
