4//! CASA-compatible MeasurementSet simulation.
5//!
6//! This crate is the Rust side of CASA's `simulator` tool (`sm`) and of the
7//! `simobserve`, `simanalyze` and `simalma` tasks
8//! (`docs/simulation/simulation-plan-20261020.md`, #841). Prediction goes
9//! through the imaging measurement operator, so simulation and imaging share
10//! one forward model.
11//!
12//! | CASA | casa-rs |
13//! |---|---|
14//! | the `sm` tool's session (`open`, the setters, `observe`, `close`) | [`session::Simulator`] |
15//! | `sm.predict` with `GridFT` or `WProjectFT` (`setoptions(ftmachine='ft' or 'wproject')`) | [`Simulator::predict`], [`Simulator::set_options`], [`predict::FtMachine`]; one row at a time, [`predict::ModelPredictor`] |
16//! | `sm.predict` with `MosaicFTNew` for ALMA and ACA (`setoptions(ftmachine='mosaic')`) | [`Simulator::predict`], [`predict::FtMachine::Mosaic`] |
17//! | the model image `sm.predict` reads | [`predict::SkyModel`] |
18//! | `sm.setvp(dovp=True)` with an Airy voltage pattern | [`Simulator::set_vp`], [`predict::PrimaryBeam`] |
19//! | `sm.setseed`, `sm.setnoise(mode='simplenoise')`, `sm.corrupt`, `sm.reset` | [`Simulator::set_seed`], [`Simulator::set_noise`], [`Simulator::corrupt`], [`Simulator::reset`] |
20//! | `simutil.readantenna(antab)` | [`readantenna::read_antenna_config`] |
21//!
22//! CASA's `sm` has no execution controls; a session's memory budget, workers
23//! and backend ([`ExecutionControls`], [`SessionResources`]) are the Python
24//! tool's constructor arguments. `close` predicts a model on the CPU or the
25//! host's Metal device (on the CPU only through `WProjectFT`) and draws
26//! noise on the CPU. Where the session departs
27//! from CASA is recorded in `docs/CASA (C++) bugs.md`.
