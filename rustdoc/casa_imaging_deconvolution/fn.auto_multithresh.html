Expand description

The next auto-multithreshold mask of one plane of shape pixels from plane’s residual and valid pixels and the mask it inherits, inherited (x-major), smoothed and pruned by beam: the plane’s step of SDMaskHandler::autoMaskByMultiThreshold, the plane its own channel block. Its new support (x-major) and its row of CASA’s automask summary; the channel stop rule (skipChannels) and which planes share a beam are ImageDomainReconstructionMaskPlans::materialize’s.

It holds at most auto_multithresh_bytes while it runs.

§Errors

Shapes disagree, the residual is not finite, or the controls or beam are invalid.
