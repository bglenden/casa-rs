pub fn weights_from_rows(

Expand description

SDGrid::pickWeights from WEIGHT, [spectrum][correlation]: each spectrum’s weight, the same in each of its channels channels ([spectrum][channel]). One correlation’s weight is taken as it is; two correlations’ w₀ and w₁ combine as the weight of their mean, 4 w₀ w₁ / (w₀ + w₁), and as 0 when their sum is zero (CAS-9957).

§Errors

Weights of more than two correlations: CASA refuses to image full-Stokes data.
