pub fn box_muller(first: u32, second: u32) -> (f32, f32)
Expand description

Two independent standard normals from two uniform 32-bit words: (r·cos θ, r·sin θ) with r = √(−2 ln u₁), u₁ = (first + 1)·2⁻³² in (0, 1], and θ = 2π·second·2⁻³².

Evaluated in f32 on the words themselves: the logarithm splits u₁ into exponent and mantissa with integer operations and sums a short atanh series, and the angle’s octant is the word’s top three bits, so the sine and cosine need only short polynomials on [0, π/4] and no range reduction. Only correctly rounded IEEE-754 operations are used, none fused, so the result is bitwise the same on every platform. Each normal is within 6·2⁻²⁴ of the double-precision Box–Muller of the same words, relatively. first = u32::MAX (u₁ = 1) gives a radius of −0, and first = 0 the largest, √(64 ln 2) correctly rounded.

The noise CellNoise::add_to_row adds is these normals scaled by σ, as scaled_draw computes them.
