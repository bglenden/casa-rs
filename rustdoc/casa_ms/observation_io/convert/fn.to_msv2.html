Expand description

Convert the store at source (MSv2 or MSv4) into a new MeasurementSet v2 at destination: a CreationPlan over the one input, written as Destination::New.

The selected data groups (ConversionOptions::data_groups) become DATA (base), CORRECTED_DATA (corrected) and MODEL_DATA (model), sharing the first group’s flags, weights and UVW. Partitions sharing a spectral window, polarization or intents share their subtable rows. Every grid cell becomes one row; padding becomes a row whose every FLAG is set, with NaN data, weights and UVW, the grid’s interval, no exposure and FLAG_ROW false (its flags already exclude it), as D23 decided. With PaddingRows::Omitted, padding becomes no row, which reproduces an original sparse MSv2. A field that follows an ephemeris gets the table casacore reads (FIELD/EPHEM<id>_<name>.tab) and its EPHEMERIS_ID, its PHASE_DIR the offset from the body and its TIME the epoch its catalogue entry states, or else its first time, the table stamped ConversionOptions::creation_date. SIGMA is derived from the weights, a loss the caller must accept as LossKind::Sigma (Loss::SigmaDerived) unless the source’s stored SIGMA is already 1/√WEIGHT. Names are the source’s: an MSv4 converted from an MSv2 holds XRADIO’s suffixed names (NAME_FIELDID, NAME_STATION, …), so a round trip renames.
