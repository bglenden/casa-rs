Expand description

Calculate an IGRF sample on a sphere offset by offset_m from the observer’s geocentric radius. The F Jones calibration uses this signed shell offset, including when it models a surface below the observer.

§Errors

The frame lacks position or epoch, or the direction or field model fails.
