Expand description

Clear the virtual models of fields, or only their entries’ combinations of spws, as CASA’s VisModelData::clearModel(ms, field, spw) does before tclean writes a virtual model of that selection:

  • with no field, every field’s model (prepare_write for MODEL_DATA, clearModel(ms)), whatever spws names;
  • with no spectral window, each named field’s model, its record, its key and its pointers;
  • otherwise each named field’s record loses the combinations of a named field and a named window (removeSpw); a record left with entries is filed again under its key, and one left with none is removed with its SOURCE pointer (CASA keeps a MAIN pointer then).

The fields are visited in turn, each seeing what the ones before left, as CASA visits them. fields and spws are the identifiers CASA’s MSSelection finds for the field and spw expressions.

§Errors

Any error reading a record or locking and writing SOURCE or MAIN.
