2//! Counter-based Gaussian noise for visibility cells: σ times a standard
3//! normal added to every real and imaginary part of a cell, each draw a
4//! pure function of a stream key and the sample's place in the data.
5//!
6//! # A draw
7//!
8//! A draw is Philox4x32-10 ([`philox4x32_10`]; Salmon, Moraes, Dror and
9//! Shaw, SC11) of a 128-bit counter under a 64-bit key: four 32-bit words.
10//! The counter is the row (its low word, then its high word), the channel
11//! and the correlation pair (correlations 0 and 1 are pair 0, 2 and 3
12//! pair 1). Words 0 and 1 make correlation `2·pair`'s (real, imaginary)
13//! normals, words 2 and 3 correlation `2·pair + 1`'s. The key names the
14//! stream and belongs to the caller: casa-simulation, for one, keys thermal
15//! noise by the term's seed and how many times the term has been applied.
16//! Distinct keys give independent streams, and one key the same values
17//! every time.
18//!
19//! Philox keeps no state, so the noise of a sample depends only on (key,
20//! row, channel, correlation): it is bitwise the same in any order, for any
21//! batch size, on any number of workers and on any backend that computes
22//! the same arithmetic.
23//!
24//! Normals come from Box–Muller on a pair of words ([`box_muller`]): with
25//! `u₁ = (w₀ + 1)·2⁻³²` in (0, 1] and `θ = 2π·w₁·2⁻³²`, the pair
26//! `√(−2 ln u₁)·(cos θ, sin θ)` is two independent standard normals. They are
27//! computed in `f32`, with the logarithm, sine and cosine evaluated on the
28//! words themselves rather than by the platform's `libm`: only IEEE-754
29//! additions, multiplications, one division and a square root, all
30//! correctly rounded and none fused, so a draw is bitwise the same on every
31//! platform. The noise added is `(r·σ)·cos θ` and `(r·σ)·sin θ`
32//! ([`scaled_draw`]): scaling the radius once saves a multiplication per
33//! sample over scaling each normal, with the same number of roundings.
34//!
35//! # Accuracy
36//!
37//! The draws replace the double-precision draws the same words gave before
38//! (#855; the tests keep that code as an oracle). With `ε = 2⁻²⁴`, the unit
39//! roundoff of `f32`, the largest errors against it over all 2³² words are
40//! 2.003ε for the radius and 2.892ε for the sine and the cosine, relatively.
41//! The radius depends on the first word only and the angle on the second
42//! only, so a standard normal, rounded once more, is within
43//! `2.003ε + 2.892ε + ε < 6ε` of the double-precision normal, relatively,
44//! for every pair of words. Over 2³³ normals the largest error is 5.18ε, and
45//! 56 % of them are the double-precision normal rounded to `f32`. Scaling
46//! by σ, as `(r·σ)·cos θ` rather than `(r·cos θ)·σ`, adds one rounding.
47//! Against noise of standard deviation σ the difference is of order 10⁻⁷σ,
48//! below what an `f32` visibility resolves.
49//!
50//! Each bound is relative down to the smallest values, because each part is
51//! computed from exact integers where precision matters: near u₁ = 1 the
52//! logarithm takes `1 − u₁` as an integer, and the angle within its octant
53//! is taken from the word's low bits. The largest draw is `√(64 ln 2)`
54//! ≈ 6.66 standard deviations (6.660437, that value correctly rounded to
55//! `f32`, at `w₀ = 0`); an exact normal exceeds it about once in 4·10⁹.
56//!
57//! # Drawing channels together
58//!
59//! A row's draws are made sixteen channels at a time: the counters of one
60//! correlation pair in consecutive channels differ only in their channel
61//! word, so the generator and Box–Muller run on all of them in step, in
62//! vector registers (Philox's multiplies with NEON intrinsics on aarch64).
63//! Every lane performs exactly the operations of a draw made alone, in the
64//! same order and with nothing fused, so the batching changes the speed and
65//! not one bit of the noise. The tests check this against the same draws
66//! made one at a time ([`philox4x32_10`] and [`scaled_draw`]), check both
67//! against the double-precision oracle within the bounds above, and hold a
68//! few draws and one cell to known bits, so that a platform that rounds
69//! differently fails.

88/// Channels drawn together. On an Apple M2, sixteen lanes (four 128-bit
89/// registers per `f32` value) ran 12–15 % faster than eight and 8–17 %
90/// faster than thirty-two (#855): fewer leave the latency of the
91/// polynomials, the division and the square root exposed; more spill
92/// Philox's words and the polynomials' coefficients and running sums out of
93/// the 32 vector registers. For the double-precision draws before them,
94/// eight lanes, also four registers per value, were fastest.
95const LANES: usize = 16;

97/// Gaussian noise of standard deviation σ for the cells of one stream: a
98/// 64-bit Philox key and σ.
99///
100/// [`Self::add_to_row`] adds σ times an independent standard normal to the
101/// real and the imaginary part of every sample of a row's cell. Sample
102/// (channel `c`, correlation `k`) of row `r` takes the words Philox4x32-10
103/// ([`philox4x32_10`]) gives the counter
104/// `[r as u32, (r >> 32) as u32, c as u32, (k / 2) as u32]` under the key:
105/// words 0 and 1 when `k` is even, 2 and 3 when it is odd, made into
106/// `(r·σ)·cos θ` and `(r·σ)·sin θ` by [`scaled_draw`]. The noise is
107/// therefore a pure function of the key, σ and the sample's place, the same
108/// whichever rows are drawn, in whatever order and on whichever thread; the
109/// [module documentation](crate::cell_noise) gives the generator, the
110/// normals and their accuracy.
111#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy, PartialEq)]
112pub struct CellNoise {
113    /// Philox's 64-bit key: which stream.
114    key: [u32; 2],
115    /// σ of each real and imaginary draw, in the cell's units.
116    sigma: f32,
117}
118
119impl CellNoise {
120    /// The stream `key` (Philox's 64-bit key) adding σ = `sigma` per real
121    /// and imaginary draw.
122    ///
123    /// Any key is valid; keys that differ in any bit give independent
124    /// streams. A caller with several kinds of noise under one seed keeps
125    /// their keys disjoint itself (casa-simulation reserves the top byte of
126    /// the second word for the purpose).
127    #[must_use]
128    pub const fn new(key: [u32; 2], sigma: f32) -> Self {
129        Self { key, sigma }
130    }
131
132    /// The stream's Philox key.
133    #[must_use]
134    pub const fn key(&self) -> [u32; 2] {
135        self.key
136    }
137
138    /// σ of each real and imaginary draw.
139    #[must_use]
140    pub const fn sigma(&self) -> f32 {
141        self.sigma
142    }
143
144    /// Add σ·N to every sample of the cell of row `row`:
145    /// `correlations × channels` samples, correlation fastest. An
146    /// autocorrelation row gets real draws only; its imaginary parts are
147    /// left as they are.
148    ///
149    /// Each draw of the counter (row, channel, pair) gives four words: the
150    /// first two make correlation `2·pair`'s (real, imaginary) normals, the
151    /// last two correlation `2·pair + 1`'s, when the row has it. The draws
152    /// are made sixteen channels at a time; a last, partial batch draws the
153    /// full width and keeps the channels the cell has. Samples past the last
154    /// whole channel are left alone, and so is the whole cell when σ is 0 or
155    /// `correlations` is 0.
156    pub fn add_to_row(
157        &self,
158        row: u64,
159        autocorrelation: bool,
160        correlations: usize,
161        cell: &mut [Complex32],
162    ) {
163        // Adding σ·N with σ = 0 changes nothing but the sign of a zero.
164        if self.sigma == 0.0 || correlations == 0 {
165            return;
166        }
167        self.add_shapes(
168            row,
169            autocorrelation,
170            correlations,
171            Uniform(self.sigma),
172            cell,
173        );
174    }
175
176    /// [`Self::add_to_row`] with σ scaled per channel: channel `c`'s samples
177    /// get σ·`channel_scale[c]` (a noise whose σ depends on frequency, as a
178    /// system temperature does). The draws are [`Self::add_to_row`]'s, so a
179    /// scale of 1 everywhere adds bit for bit the same noise; another scale
180    /// gives its unit draws times σ·`channel_scale[c]` to the rounding of
181    /// that product, which scales each draw's radius.
182    ///
183    /// # Panics
184    ///
185    /// When `channel_scale` has fewer values than the cell has whole
186    /// channels.
187    pub fn add_to_row_scaled(
188        &self,
189        row: u64,
190        autocorrelation: bool,
191        correlations: usize,
192        channel_scale: &[f32],
193        cell: &mut [Complex32],
194    ) {
195        if self.sigma == 0.0 || correlations == 0 {
196            return;
197        }
198        assert!(
199            channel_scale.len() >= cell.len() / correlations,
200            "a scale for every channel of the cell"
201        );
202        self.add_shapes(
203            row,
204            autocorrelation,
205            correlations,
206            Scaled {
207                sigma: self.sigma,
208                channel_scale,
209            },
210            cell,
211        );
212    }
213
214    /// The common cross-correlation shapes, each compiled with its
215    /// correlation count fixed so that the additions into the cell
216    /// vectorise; every arm runs the same code.
217    #[inline(always)]
218    fn add_shapes<S: LaneSigma>(
219        &self,
220        row: u64,
221        autocorrelation: bool,
222        correlations: usize,
223        sigma: S,
224        cell: &mut [Complex32],
225    ) {
226        match (correlations, autocorrelation) {
227            (1, false) => self.add_batches(row, 1, false, sigma, cell),
228            (2, false) => self.add_batches(row, 2, false, sigma, cell),
229            (4, false) => self.add_batches(row, 4, false, sigma, cell),
230            _ => self.add_batches(row, correlations, autocorrelation, sigma, cell),
231        }
232    }
233
234    /// [`Self::add_to_row`] in batches of [`LANES`] channels, and a last,
235    /// partial batch.
236    #[inline(always)]
237    fn add_batches<S: LaneSigma>(
238        &self,
239        row: u64,
240        correlations: usize,
241        autocorrelation: bool,
242        sigma: S,
243        cell: &mut [Complex32],
244    ) {
245        let batch = Batch {
246            key: self.key,
247            sigma,
248            row,
249            correlations,
250            autocorrelation,
251        };
252        let channels = cell.len() / correlations;
253        let mut batches = cell[..channels * correlations].chunks_exact_mut(LANES * correlations);
254        for (index, samples) in (&mut batches).enumerate() {
255            batch.add(index * LANES, samples, LANES);
256        }
257        let tail = batches.into_remainder();
258        if !tail.is_empty() {
259            batch.add_partial(channels - channels % LANES, tail, channels % LANES);
260        }
261    }
262}

264/// σ of each of a batch's [`LANES`] channels.
265trait LaneSigma: Copy {
266    /// σ of the channels from `first`.
267    fn lanes(self, first: usize) -> [f32; LANES];
268}
269
270/// One σ for every channel.
271#[derive(Clone, Copy)]
272struct Uniform(f32);
273
274impl LaneSigma for Uniform {
275    #[inline(always)]
276    fn lanes(self, _first: usize) -> [f32; LANES] {
277        [self.0; LANES]
278    }
279}

281/// σ times each channel's scale; channels past the scales (a partial
282/// batch's draws beyond the cell) get 0.
283#[derive(Clone, Copy)]
284struct Scaled<'a> {
285    sigma: f32,
286    channel_scale: &'a [f32],
287}
288
289impl LaneSigma for Scaled<'_> {
290    #[inline(always)]
291    fn lanes(self, first: usize) -> [f32; LANES] {
292        std::array::from_fn(|lane| {
293            self.channel_scale
294                .get(first + lane)
295                .map_or(0.0, |scale| self.sigma * scale)
296        })
297    }
298}

300/// What every batch of one row's noise shares.
301struct Batch<S> {
302    /// The stream key.
303    key: philox::Key,
304    /// σ of each draw.
305    sigma: S,
306    /// The row.
307    row: u64,
308    /// Correlations per channel.
309    correlations: usize,
310    /// An autocorrelation row, which gets real draws only.
311    autocorrelation: bool,
312}
313
314impl<S: LaneSigma> Batch<S> {
315    /// Add the noise of the `lanes` channels from `first`, at most
316    /// [`LANES`], to their `samples`: the draws of each correlation pair in
317    /// all the channels, then the additions, both correlations of the pair
318    /// together.
319    #[inline(always)]
320    fn add(&self, first: usize, samples: &mut [Complex32], lanes: usize) {
321        let correlations = self.correlations;
322        let sigma = self.sigma.lanes(first);
323        for pair in 0..correlations.div_ceil(2) {
324            let words = philox::philox4x32_10_lanes(counters(self.row, first, pair), self.key);
325            let both = correlations - 2 * pair >= 2;
326            let [re0, im0] = scaled_draws(&words[0], &words[1], &sigma);
327            let [re1, im1] = if both {
328                scaled_draws(&words[2], &words[3], &sigma)
329            } else {
330                [[0.0; LANES]; 2]
331            };
332            for lane in 0..lanes {
333                let at = lane * correlations + 2 * pair;
334                samples[at].re += re0[lane];
335                if both {
336                    samples[at + 1].re += re1[lane];
337                }
338                if !self.autocorrelation {
339                    samples[at].im += im0[lane];
340                    if both {
341                        samples[at + 1].im += im1[lane];
342                    }
343                }
344            }
345        }
346    }
347
348    /// [`Self::add`] for a row's last, partial batch: one copy for every
349    /// shape, since it runs at most once a row.
350    #[inline(never)]
351    fn add_partial(&self, first: usize, samples: &mut [Complex32], lanes: usize) {
352        self.add(first, samples, lanes);
353    }
354}

356/// The counters of one correlation pair's draws in the [`LANES`] channels
357/// from `first`: the row (two words), the channel, and the correlation pair
358/// (correlations 0 and 1 are pair 0, 2 and 3 pair 1).
359fn counters(row: u64, first: usize, pair: usize) -> philox::Lanes<LANES> {
360    [
361        [row as u32; LANES],
362        [(row >> 32) as u32; LANES],
363        std::array::from_fn(|lane| (first + lane) as u32),
364        [pair as u32; LANES],
365    ]
366}

368/// The real and imaginary noise of `LANES` samples from their two words
369/// each: Box–Muller's `(r·σ)·cos θ` and `(r·σ)·sin θ`, in `f32`. Scaling
370/// the radius once saves a multiplication per sample over scaling each
371/// normal, with the same number of roundings.
372///
373/// Each lane computes the products as a draw made alone does
374/// ([`scaled_draw`]), but in two passes, the radii and then the angles:
375/// each pass's polynomial coefficients and running sums then fit the vector
376/// registers together, which keeps more lanes' arithmetic in flight.
377#[inline(always)]
378fn scaled_draws<const LANES: usize>(
379    first: &[u32; LANES],
380    second: &[u32; LANES],
381    sigma: &[f32; LANES],
382) -> [[f32; LANES]; 2] {
383    let mut radii = [0.0; LANES];
384    for ((radius, &word), &sigma) in radii.iter_mut().zip(first).zip(sigma) {
385        *radius = normal::radius(word) * sigma;
386    }
387    let mut draws = [[0.0; LANES]; 2];
388    for lane in 0..LANES {
389        let (sin, cos) = normal::sin_cos_turn(second[lane]);
390        draws[0][lane] = radii[lane] * cos;
391        draws[1][lane] = radii[lane] * sin;
392    }
393    draws
394}

396/// The four standard normals of one draw, slot 0's (real, imaginary) then
397/// slot 1's, as [`CellNoise::add_to_row`] uses them.
398#[cfg(test)]
399fn standard_normals(noise: &CellNoise, row: u64, channel: usize, pair: usize) -> [f32; 4] {
400    let counter = [row as u32, (row >> 32) as u32, channel as u32, pair as u32];
401    let words = philox::philox4x32_10(counter, noise.key());
402    let (a, b) = box_muller(words[0], words[1]);
403    let (c, d) = box_muller(words[2], words[3]);
404    [a, b, c, d]
