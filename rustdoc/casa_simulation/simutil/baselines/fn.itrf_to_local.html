simutil.itrf2loc(x, y, z, cx, cy, cz): positions relative to centre, as [east, north, up] metres in the plane tangent to the WGS84 ellipsoid at the centre’s latitude and longitude. (CASA returns the three as lat, lon, el.) The centre’s latitude and longitude come from casa-rs’s xyz2long, which corrects CASA’s (see crate::readantenna::geodesy); the rotation is CASA’s.
