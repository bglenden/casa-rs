Direct, reusable two-dimensional FFTW plans for real and complex planes.

The native FFTW library is GPL-2.0-or-later. Distributions linking these plans must satisfy GPLv3 terms for the combined program.

The most C heap FFTW keeps for one plan pair (twiddles and buffers): under 0.7 MB is measured for transforms up to 8192², the prime 8191² real transform keeping the most (the law a_plan_pair_keeps_at_most_its_bytes).
