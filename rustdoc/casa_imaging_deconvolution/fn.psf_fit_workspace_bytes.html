pub fn psf_fit_workspace_bytes(shape: [usize; 2]) -> u64

Heap envelope for the bounded two-pass lobe extraction, excluding its borrowed full PSF. The Gaussian solve uses fixed-size stack matrices.

The lobe search patch is at most 2·20 + 1 pixels per axis; the fit window is the CASA window ([fit_window]) of a whole patch around the image centre, where CASA’s PSF peaks. On a non-square plane squaring by index makes that window span the gap between the two centre pixels.
