Source
Expand description

The products of an imaging run (plan section 5.7).

This crate turns a major cycle’s final normal state and model into the members of the problem’s product list: normalization, restoring-beam fitting, restoration, Taylor products, PB and pbcor, validity and metadata. It writes each member in bounded windows to private staging beside its CASA image target, then replaces each target atomically.
