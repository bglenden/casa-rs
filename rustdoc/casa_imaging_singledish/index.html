Single-dish image-plane gridding, as CASA’s SDGrid does it for tsdimaging and for the simulator’s total-power prediction.

Single-dish (total-power) imaging places each spectrum at the image pixel of its pointing and spreads it over the pixels around with a radial kernel; there is no FFT, no uv coordinate and no gridding correction. Prediction is the adjoint: the model sampled through the same kernel at each pointing. ADR-0016 records this as its own operator family, beside the four interferometric kernel sets of casa-imaging-operator.

Reading the spectra and pointings from a MeasurementSet, placing a pointing on the image (SDGrid::getXYPos), and writing the products are the task’s (#938).

The gridder holds its two grids whole, as CASA does, under an admission of TotalPowerGrid::bytes, and grids the image’s channels in parallel on the imaging runtime’s worker team.

beam_sampling
The sampling tsdimaging gives the theoretical beam (set_beam_size), from a raster’s intervals in arcsec: both when they differ by more than 1%, else one; the along-scan one alone when the across-scan one is invalid (below 1 mas, or not finite). None, and CASA sets no beam, when the along-scan interval is invalid (below 2.2 mas, or not finite), whatever the other: CASA logs that it uses the across-scan interval, but marks the sampling invalid.
