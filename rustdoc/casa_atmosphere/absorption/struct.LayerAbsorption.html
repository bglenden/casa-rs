Expand description

One layer’s refractivity per unit path at one frequency, by species, (rad/m, 1/m): everything ATM’s RefractiveIndexProfile keeps.

The O2, H2O and O3 totals are ATM’s own sums of their parts, so they equal ATM’s to the bit.

pub fn of_layer(layer: &Layer, frequency_ghz: f64) -> Self

The species’ refractivities in layer at frequency_ghz, as ATM’s mkRefractiveIndexProfile forms them: each species once, the O2, H2O and O3 totals as ATM sums them, and nothing for a gas the layer has none of.
