casa_ms::observation_io

Module convert 

Source
Expand description

Converting between MeasurementSet v2 and MSv4 through the observation boundary: the source is read as batches like any consumer reads it, and the destination is created from those batches and the source’s catalogue (to_msv4(), to_msv2()).

A conversion first inventories what the destination cannot hold (a Loss) and refuses, before any byte is written, every loss the caller did not accept (ConversionOptions::accept), as a CreationPlan does; converting into an MSv2 is a plan over one input. What it keeps it keeps exactly: every acquisition, its flags (row flags folded in), weights, UVW and time auxiliaries, and every selected data group. Padding, a grid cell no acquisition filled, stays padding: an MSv4 holds it as a NaN weight, and an MSv2 as a flagged row of NaN data and weights, or, by the declared policy that reproduces an original sparse MSv2, as no row (PaddingRows).
