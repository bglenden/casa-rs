Struct BufferPool 

Source 
pub struct BufferPool { /* private fields */ }
Expand description

A bounded set of BatchBuffers that owned batches are filled into: at most capacity buffers of at most envelope bytes each, free or lent, whatever batches, roles and pieces they are filled with. That product is what the caller admits for the pool; the envelope for a traversal is its estimate’s peak_buffer_bytes, and each live cursor’s decode scratch is admitted separately.

Traversal::next_owned takes a free buffer only for a batch, and waits while none is free: that wait is the backpressure a slow consumer applies to the producer. A FilledBatch gives its buffer back when it is dropped, so a batch shared through an Arc comes back when its last user lets go. Cloning the pool shares it, so several cursors, or a producer and the work units packing pieces, can draw on one bound.

Buffers are allocated as they are first filled and keep their capacity after that, within the envelope: a fill whose batch needs more is refused before anything is allocated, a fill frees what a buffer kept from earlier fills when keeping it would exceed the envelope, and a returned buffer over it is emptied.

A take waits for a free buffer without limit, unless the handle was made Self::waiting_at_most a duration: it then fails with ObservationError::PoolExhausted instead. Without a limit, a thread that takes from a pool must not also be the only holder of every batch the pool has lent, or it waits forever.

pub fn waiting_at_most(&self, limit: Duration) -> Self

A handle on the same buffers whose takes wait at most limit for a free one (zero: never wait). A runtime that must stay cancellable takes with a short limit and checks for cancellation between tries; Traversal::next_owned refused this way has consumed nothing, so asking again delivers the same batch.

Fill a free buffer, waiting for one, through fill; the buffer goes back to the pool if fill fails or finishes no batch.

§Errors

ObservationError::PoolExhausted when the handle’s wait limit passed with no buffer free (fill is then not called); as fill (which refuses a batch over the envelope through BatchBuffers::begin); or ObservationError::InvalidBatch when it returned without finishing a batch.

Copy batch (typically a compute window of a larger batch) into a free buffer of this pool, waiting for one (BatchBuffers::pack): a piece with storage of its own, and the payload elements copied.
