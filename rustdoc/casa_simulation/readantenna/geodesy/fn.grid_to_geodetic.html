CASA’s tmgeod(n, e, eps, cm, fe, sf, so, r, v0, v2, v4, v6, fn, er, esq): northing n and easting e to [lat, lon, conv, kp], the geodetic latitude and west-positive longitude (radians), the convergence and the point scale factor, with the zone constants of zone_constants and central meridian cm, false easting fe, false northing fn, scale factor sf, equatorial radius er and eccentricity squared esq.
