casa_simulation::session

Module resources 

Source
Expand description

The host, memory, workers and device a session runs on.

A session has no budget or worker model of its own (plan §4, Prediction): close admits its memory against the imaging runtime’s ResourcePolicy on the detected HostResources, and runs its geometry and noise on the runtime’s worker team.

The controls are the Python tool’s constructor arguments (ADR-0019 item 3), ExecutionControls:

  • with neither a memory budget nor a worker count, the session takes the balanced policy: three quarters of the host’s free memory and of its threads, at most its performance cores, except that a close that predicts on Metal streams on every thread but the writer’s (SessionResources::close_cores);
  • with either, an explicit policy whose other half is the balanced policy’s on the host.

The budget caps everything close holds at once: the MeasurementSet writer gets half of what the policy leaves free, and the batch being filled, its geometry and the worker scratch come out of the rest (write::resources). The worker count is the runtime’s: threads kept busy, the MeasurementSet writer’s among them.

The backend is where a session predicts its model (SessionResources::prediction_backend); noise never runs on a device, so a session that predicts nothing writes the same MeasurementSet whatever the request. Metal predicts through the standard GridFT kernel set, which the imaging runtime’s Metal laws and the simulator’s own (tests/backends.rs) cover, so Auto takes the host’s Metal device whenever there is one, except for a session that projects w (setoptions(ftmachine='wproject')): close predicts that on the CPU, and predict refuses it on an explicit Metal.
