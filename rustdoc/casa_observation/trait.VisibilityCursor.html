Expand description

A bound traversal of one partition of an AccessRequest.

An adapter implements Self::step and Self::fill; consumers call Self::next, which lends the filled buffers as a VisibilityBatch, or Self::next_owned, which fills a pooled buffer and hands it over as a FilledBatch.
