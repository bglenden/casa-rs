pub fn compile( problem: &CompiledProblem, catalogue: &Arc<Catalogue>, measures: Arc<dyn MeasuresProvider>, phase_table: Option<Arc<FieldEphemeris>>, ) -> Result<Self, BatchGeometryError>

The geometry of problem on the observation catalogue describes, converting with measures. phase_table is the ephemeris table a moving phase centre names, when it names one rather than a body (CASA’s phasecenter takes both): the caller reads it, since a law names a table and a body alike.

§Errors

BatchGeometryError for a catalogue without what geometry needs, a centre law or domain it does not take (among them a moving centre that is not TRACKFIELD and that Measures does not name or has no model of, without a table), a table for a centre that does not move, or a failed conversion.

pub fn cached_bytes(&self) -> usize

At most the bytes the plan has come to hold beyond its compiled state: the parallactic angles of every (time, time scale, field) a batch with rows has asked for, one per antenna, kept for the observation’s other partitions, and the table that finds them. Once a traversal has read every batch it holds, for each distinct (time, field) with rows, 8 bytes an antenna and at most about 80 bytes of table: the size step 4’s admission charges. The table’s share is bounded from the standard table’s layout; a counting law (batch_geometry_memory) holds the bound to what is allocated.

Source

pub fn cache_bound(&self, keys: usize) -> usize

At most what Self::cached_bytes reports once a traversal has read rows at keys distinct (time, field) pairs, known before any batch is read: the source’s admission charges it. Zero when the problem needs no parallactic angle.

pub fn geometry_bytes( &self, samples: usize, times: usize, channels: usize, ) -> usize

The bytes a BatchGeometry holds for a batch of samples samples over times times of channels channels (BatchGeometry::capacity_bytes after Self::apply on the largest such batch).

pub fn aperture_classes(&self) -> Vec<AntennaResponseClass>

CASA’s aperture classes of the observation’s antennas, sorted and without repeats: the dishes a heterogeneous mosaic’s kernel set keys on (HetArrayConvFunc::findAntennaSizes).

Source

pub fn frequency_factor( &self, frame: FrequencyRef, field: FieldId, time_mjd_s: f64, scale: EpochRef, ) -> Result<f64, BatchGeometryError>

The factor that takes a frequency in frame, observed towards field field at time_mjd_s on scale, to the output frame: Self::apply gives each channel its native frequency times this factor, to rounding. A source sizes a wave’s channel window from it without reading a batch.

§Errors

As Self::apply’s frequency conversion: a conversion into or out of the rest frame without a source velocity, a field’s ephemeris that does not cover the time, or a failed conversion.

Source

pub fn apply( &self, batch: &VisibilityBatch<'_>, rows: &[bool], pointing: Option<&PointingSeries>, traversal: &mut GeometryTraversal, out: &mut BatchGeometry, ) -> Result<(), BatchGeometryError>

The geometry of batch’s row samples (rows, one flag a sample) into out; pointing holds the batch partition’s samples over its times when Self::reads_pointing.

§Errors

BatchGeometryError for a batch of another observation or without what geometry reads, frequencies to convert into or out of the rest frame, a failed conversion, or a pointing law that gives no direction.

§Panics

If rows does not have one flag for each sample.
