Expand description

Add entry to the virtual model of its fields, as CASA’s VisModelData::putModel (version 2) does: the record already filed under the entry’s key, else the record a SOURCE row in use for the first field’s source holds, gains the entry after its others when incremental or when it had a record to add to; otherwise (a new record, not incremental) the record is the entry alone, and the images the old record named are deleted. Without incremental an entry of the old record covering one of the new combinations is removed first (addToRec). The record is then filed as the module states, and every field the entry covers points to it.

tclean adds its models incrementally, each image domain’s in turn, after clearing the selected fields’ models (clear_virtual_model).

SOURCE and then MAIN are written in place under casacore’s write lock, each only when it changes, as crate::scratch_columns writes them.

§Errors

MsError::InvalidInput for an entry that names no field, and when the record to add to is not of version 2: CASA refuses to combine with it (“please clear it before proceeding”) and writes nothing, as here. Any error reading the record or FIELD, or locking and writing SOURCE or MAIN.
