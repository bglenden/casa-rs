Expand description

An image selection and the matching coordinates and beam metadata. Pixels remain in the source and are copied in chunks when writing the result.

Fields§

§shape: Vec<usize>

Pixel shape in output axis order.

§coordinates: CoordinateSystem

Coordinates shifted to the lower bound of the output.

§coordinate_record: Option<RecordValue>

Native coordinate representation when it differs from the typed model.
