casacore’s quantity string reader.

Quantity::read is the Rust counterpart of casacore’s Quantity::read(Quantity&, const String&) (readQuantity in Quantum2.cc), the reader behind QuantumHolder::fromString and so behind every quantity string a CASA tool or task accepts. It tries, in order:

  1. an angle in MVAngle::read form: 12h30m00.0, 45d00m00, -30.00.00.5 (dotted degrees), 12:30:00 (a time-angle, 15 degrees per hour) or 10d (degrees), giving degrees;
  2. a date-time in MVTime::read form: 2020/01/01/12:00:00, 2020-01-01T12:00:00+02:00, 01Jan2020/12:00, 15-Jan-99, 2020/01/15.5, 58849/12:00 (an MJD day and a time), today or now, giving days (MJD for calendar dates);
  3. a value and a unit: 10s, 1.4GHz, 50 MHz, .5, Jy (zero Jy).

Each step keeps casacore’s quirks, which scripts rely on: 12h is 12 hours but 12h30m is 187.5 degrees, 10d is 10 degrees but 10.d is 10 days, and dates use casacore’s calendar formula (Julian before 1582-10-15, no range checks, so 2020-02-30 is 1 March).

The unit after a value is checked with this crate’s unit parser and named as casacore’s Unit names it (" MHz" becomes MHz, km s-1 becomes km.s-1). casacore’s unit grammar is wider than the parser’s in a few corners (h:, a leading digit or repeated signs before a unit); those strings are rejected here.

Other readers in the workspace parse subsets of these forms for their own inputs: parse_sexagesimal in casa-imaging-application’s phase-centre compiler and parse_datetime_to_mjd in casa-tables’ TaQL functions.

Functions§

casacore_unit_name
casacore’s Unit::check renaming: ** and ^ dropped, * as ., blanks as . (or / when a run of blanks holds one), // as ., runs of dots collapsed (to / when they hold one) and leading and trailing dots removed. casacore names every unit it builds this way, so m.s-1 (UnitDim’s output) is m.s-1.
unique_prefix_index
casacore’s MUString::minimaxNC: the index of the name input matches, case-insensitively, either exactly or as the only name sharing its first min(input, name) characters. casacore matches measure reference codes (MEpoch::getType and the others) and month names this way, so j20 is J2000 and U is ambiguous among UT1, UT2, UTC and UT.
