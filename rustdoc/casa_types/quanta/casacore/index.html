casa_types::quanta

Module casacore 

Source
Expand description

casacore’s Quantum<Double> arithmetic, and the strings CASA’s quanta tool (qa) writes, for code that must reproduce CASA’s numbers to the last bit: casars’ qa tool, and simutil’s pointing lists, whose printed digits follow every rounding of casacore’s arithmetic.

Quantity’s operators round differently:

  • Sums here divide the second value by the ratio of the unit factors (Quantum::getValue), and take the first unit ('1km' + '1m' is 1.001 km); they need units of equal dimensions.
  • Conversions multiply by that ratio (Quantum::convert). Between angles and times they go at 360 degrees a day. For other units the value is kept in the target unit times the remaining SI dimensions ('1.5km' to s is 1500 s.m.s-1). With no target they convert to SI base units.
  • Products and quotients name their unit a.b, a/(b) and (b)-1.

Unit names are normalised as casacore’s Unit::check normalises them (casacore_unit).
