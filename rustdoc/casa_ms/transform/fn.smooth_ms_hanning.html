Expand description

Smooth existing visibility, flags and weights at the specified MAIN rows.

A present weight spectrum supplies channel-dependent weights and keeps scalar WEIGHT. Otherwise WEIGHT is multiplied by 8/3. SIGMA is unchanged. Rows with fewer than three channels are left untouched.

§Errors

A required column cannot be read or written, cell shapes disagree, or the supplied lock does not protect the physical MAIN table.
