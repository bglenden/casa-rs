Convert as request says.

outputvis never lies inside vis. It must not exist, or, with overwrite, must be a MeasurementSet (v2 or v4, not a link) that does not hold vis, on a filesystem that exchanges directories, and an MSv2 no other process writes; it is held so (Replacement::hold) before anything is converted. It is replaced only once its replacement is complete: the conversion is written beside it under a hidden name, the two are exchanged in one atomic call, and the old store is removed. A conversion that fails leaves outputvis as it was. An MSv2 converted into an MSv4 is partitioned as request.partition_scheme says, its pointing carried or not, and each dataset chunked by request.chunks or, without one, as XRADIO chunks it (xradio_chunks). Every loss the output’s format imposes is taken, and each is logged as a warning and returned.

§Errors

A validation failure (nothing written) for an output not to be written or replaced, or a source the converter does not support; an execution failure for anything that failed while converting or replacing, which leaves no partial output.
