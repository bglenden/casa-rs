The smallest nonzero |σ| whose draws a Metal device makes bit for bit as the host does: 2⁻⁴⁰, about 9·10⁻¹³.

The device flushes subnormal f32 values to zero; the host keeps them. No intermediate of a draw is subnormal whatever σ is: the logarithm’s series and the octant’s polynomials are exactly zero or at least about 2⁻⁹⁸ (the logarithm’s correction at u nearest 1). A draw itself, r·σ·sin θ or r·σ·cos θ, is zero or at least r_min·s_min·|σ|, where r_min ≈ 2.2·10⁻⁵ is the smallest nonzero radius (u = 1 − 2⁻³²) and s_min ≈ 1.5·10⁻⁹ the smallest nonzero sine (one step of 2⁻²⁹ of an octant): 3·10⁻¹⁴·|σ|. From |σ| ≥ 2⁻⁵⁵ every nonzero draw is at least 2⁻¹⁰⁰. A sum of it and a normal cell then has both addends’ units in the last place at least 2⁻¹²⁴, or the cell far below the draw, so it is zero or normal. 2⁻⁴⁰ leaves a margin of 2¹⁵. Any physical noise is many orders of magnitude above it.
