The simutil analysis that simanalyze and simalma run on a simulated image (simulation Phase 6f, #841): CASA 6.7.6’s simutil.py and the analysis section of task_simanalyze.py, ported as their specification (ADR-0018).

simanalyze compares the image it made with the sky model it simulated:

  • both are flattened to one Stokes I plane (.flat, the channel mean of a cube);
  • convimage() regrids the model’s flat onto the image’s flat and convolves it with the image’s restoring beam, giving the model as the telescope would see it at full sensitivity;
  • fidelity() subtracts the image from that and divides the convolved model by the difference, floored at the difference’s median absolute deviation over √2.

Where casa-rs differs, each recorded in docs/CASA (C++) bugs.md:

  • simanalyze writes the fidelity’s floor into its image expression with %f, six decimal places: a floor under 5e-7 becomes 0, and one of 7.07e-6 becomes 7e-6. casa-rs uses the floor itself.
  • CASA’s fidelity image carries the convolved model’s Jy/beam; casa-rs gives the ratio no unit.
  • CASA computes the difference and fidelity with image expressions, whose evaluator labels any expression containing “pa” as a position angle (unit deg, Stokes axis Pangle), so a project whose path contains “pa” gets them so labelled. casa-rs computes them directly and keeps the Stokes axis.
  • statim multiplies the statistics of a Jy/beam image without a restoring beam by the pixel area in square arcseconds, and still reports them as Jy/beam. casa-rs reports the image’s own values.
  • ismstp tests uvw.all() == 0, which is true when any UVW value is zero; casa-rs tests what was meant, that all are.

The plotting parts of statim (the display range from the cumulative histogram, and the figure) go with the graphics (Phase 6i).
