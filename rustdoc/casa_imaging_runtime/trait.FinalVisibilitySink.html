Expand description

Bounded consumer of final visibility samples produced inside the paired terminal replay. Implementations may write selected visibility columns in place or retain residual-visibility products, but receive no publication authority from this interface.

fn abort(&mut self) -> Result<()>

Abort an incomplete replay and synchronously join any storage worker.

Returning guarantees that the sink no longer owns a storage lock or background worker, whether the replay failed or was cancelled.
