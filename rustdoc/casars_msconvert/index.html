msconvert: convert between MeasurementSet v2 and MSv4 (XRADIO’s processing set), in either direction, on the shared task front end.

The task is casa-rs’s own: CASA has none, and XRADIO’s convert_msv2_to_processing_set converts one way only. It is a surface over casa-ms’s converter (casa_ms::observation_io::convert), which reads the source through the observation boundary as any consumer reads it and creates the destination from those batches: every acquisition and every selected data group, flags, weights, UVW and time auxiliaries are carried exactly, and padding stays padding.

What the destination cannot hold is inventoried before anything is written. The task takes every such loss, as converting into the other format asks it to, and reports each: logged as a warning, and returned. Every conversion between the two formats loses SIGMA: MSv4 holds none, and an MSv2 made from an MSv4 states it as 1/√WEIGHT. Most also lose subtables MSv4 does not hold (HISTORY, WEATHER, …), or state in an MSv2 what an MSv4 leaves unstated, as MSv2’s defaults.

Stores are named as XRADIO names them. Without an outputvis, the output is written beside vis, named after it with .ps.zarr for an MSv4 or .ms for an MSv2 (default_outputvis): obs.ms converts to obs.ps.zarr, and obs.ps.zarr back to obs.ms. An MSv4 outputvis not ending in ps.zarr gains .ps.zarr, as XRADIO’s converter names it (processing_set_path).

An MSv4 destination is named and laid out as XRADIO’s converter lays out an MSv2, with its options: one dataset per partition, the MSv2 partitioned further by partitionscheme (XRADIO’s partition_scheme), its pointing converted unless withpointing is off (with_pointing), and by default each dataset chunked as XRADIO chunks it (xradio_chunks). Its partitions are converted nthreads at a time, by default as many as the cores and the memory allow. An MSv2 destination is written by one thread.

The task returns a record of what it did: the formats, the partitions converted and the losses taken.

Functions§

default_outputvis
The output a conversion of vis into outformat writes when none is named, by XRADIO’s convention: beside vis, named after it, its extension (.ms, .zarr or .ps.zarr, any case) replaced by .ps.zarr for a processing set or .ms for a MeasurementSet v2 (StoreFormat::extension), or that extension added to a name without one: obs.ms becomes obs.ps.zarr, and obs.ps.zarr obs.ms. None when vis names no file, or a name that is not UTF-8.
default_workers
The threads nthreads=0 takes on a host of cores cores and physical_bytes of memory: as many as half the memory holds at 1 GiB each (a conservative bound; a thread measured about 100 MB on a 1.4 GB MSv2 of 32 windows), at most one a core, and at least one. Unknown memory gives one.
