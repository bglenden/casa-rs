Assemble a portable CASA MultiMS from the input tables in order.

Members move into SUBMSS on success. Referencing a physical member twice retains both logical occurrences and moves it once. Copying subtables replaces the selected links with copies from the first member.

§Errors

A missing or incompatible member, a conflicting output, or a failed publication. On failure member moves are rolled back. If a concurrent change prevents restoration, surviving originals are retained for recovery.
