A native port of ATM, the atmospheric transmission model in CASA.

ATM (Pardo, Cernicharo & Serabyn 2001, IEEE Trans. Antennas Propag. 49, 1683) computes the opacity, phase delay and sky brightness of the Earth’s atmosphere from 1 GHz to a few THz. CASA uses it in the at tool, in sm.setnoise(mode='tsys-atm') and in sm.settrop. This crate ports the C++ library CASA ships (casatools/src/code/atmosphere/ATM, version 1.0.0) so that casa-rs computes the same numbers natively.

§The model

  1. AtmosphereProfile divides the atmosphere above a site into horizontal layers, from the ground pressure, temperature and humidity and one of ATM’s reference atmospheres (AtmosphereType). Each layer has a thickness and mean pressure, temperature, water vapour and minor-gas densities.
  2. refractivity gives each gas’s complex refractivity at a layer’s conditions: the imaginary part is the absorption coefficient, the real part the phase delay per unit path. Line species sum Van Vleck–Weisskopf profiles over ATM’s line catalogues; the water and dry continua are empirical.
  3. A SpectralGrid lists the frequencies (spectral windows of channels) at which the refractivities are evaluated.

Integrating the layer refractivities over the profile gives the zenith opacity and path delay, and radiative transfer through the layers gives the sky brightness; those build on this crate’s types.

§Agreement with the C++

The port follows ATM’s arithmetic, including its line-window tables and its known data defects (listed in docs/CASA (C++) bugs.md), so that results agree with CASA’s at tool. The tests compare every layer field and every species’ refractivity with fixtures produced by compiling CASA’s ATM sources (tools/atmosphere/build-atm-oracle.sh).

§Cost and parallelism

The refractivity of every (layer, channel) pair is independent, and all functions here are pure. A caller evaluating many channels can spread them over threads; the crate itself starts none. ATM works in double precision throughout, which rules out GPU backends without native fp64 (Apple’s).

§Units

Values are plain f64, with the unit in each name: pressures in hPa (ATM’s millibars), temperatures in kelvin, heights in metres unless the name says kilometres, frequencies in hertz in the spectral grid and in GHz in the refractivity functions (as in ATM).
