4//! The shared radio-interferometric measurement operator.
5//!
6//! This crate owns the forward operator `A` (model image to predicted
7//! visibilities) and its adjoint `A*` (weighted visibilities to unnormalised
8//! image-domain normal images) as one implementation parameterised by a
9//! convolution-function set ([`ConvolutionFunctionSet`]: the standard
10//! [`Spheroidal`] set, [`WPlanes`], the [`AwCatalog`] and the mosaic
11//! [`MosaicPb`]) and a gridding backend ([`GridBackend`]: [`CpuBackend`]
12//! here; the Metal backend in `casa-imaging-metal` grids into
13//! [`GridStorage::Device`] cells). It also owns the global weighting generation
14//! ([`WeightingGeneration`]), the spectral resampler that turns native
15//! rows into [`Placement`]s, and CASA's visibility-domain continuum fit
16//! ([`fit_and_subtract_continuum`]).
17//!
18//! The design is `docs/imaging-architecture/imaging-foundation-plan-20261007.md`
19//! section 5.3 and ADR-0016.
20//!
21//! # Conventions
22//!
23//! Grid and image planes are `[y][x]`, x fastest. Visibilities follow the
24//! MeasurementSet convention `V = ∫ I e^{+2πi(ul + vm)}`; the adjoint uses
25//! an unnormalised inverse FFT, so a source at direction cosines `(l, m)`
26//! lands on image pixel `(l/Δx, m/Δy)` from the reference pixel with the
27//! signed WCS increments. Values in a [`SampleBlock`] are pre-multiplied by
28//! the imaging weight and the phase-centre phasor `e^{iφ}`; prediction
29//! applies `e^{−iφ}`. The image correction is one real diagonal applied on
30//! both sides of the operator (CASA divides the model and the image by the
31//! same spheroidal response), so `A*` is the exact adjoint of `A` up to the
32//! polarization basis, whose image-domain conversion follows CASA's
33//! `StokesImageUtil` including its `1/2` factors and `sumwt` rule.
