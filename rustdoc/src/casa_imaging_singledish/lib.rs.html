4//! Single-dish image-plane gridding, as CASA's `SDGrid` does it for
5//! `tsdimaging` and for the simulator's total-power prediction.
6//!
7//! Single-dish (total-power) imaging places each spectrum at the image
8//! pixel of its pointing and spreads it over the pixels around with a
9//! radial kernel; there is no FFT, no uv coordinate and no gridding
10//! correction. Prediction is the adjoint: the model sampled through the
11//! same kernel at each pointing. ADR-0016 records this as its own operator
12//! family, beside the four interferometric kernel sets of
13//! `casa-imaging-operator`.
14//!
15//! | CASA | casa-rs |
16//! |---|---|
17//! | `SDGrid::init`, `SDGrid::findPBAsConvFunction` (`gridfunction`, `convsupport`, `truncate`, `gwidth`, `jwidth`) | [`GridFunction`], [`KernelTable`] |
18//! | `sgridsd`, `ogridsd` (`fgridsd.f`): a pointing's pixels | [`KernelTable::taps`] |
19//! | `dgridsd`: the model at a pointing | [`KernelTable::degrid`] |
20//! | `ggridsd`, `SDGrid::getImage`, `getWeightImage` | [`TotalPowerGrid`] |
21//! | `SDGrid::pickWeights` | [`weights_from_rows`], [`weights_from_spectra`] |
22//! | `tsdimaging`'s `do_weight_mask` | [`weight_mask_threshold`] |
23//! | `SingleDishBeamUtil::getPointingSamplingRaster` | [`raster_sampling`] |
24//! | `tsdimaging`'s `set_beam_size` sampling choice | [`beam_sampling`] |
25//! | `sdbeamutil.TheoreticalBeam`, `primaryBeamArcsec` | [`theoretical_beam_arcsec`], [`primary_beam_fwhm_arcsec`] |
26//!
27//! Reading the spectra and pointings from a MeasurementSet, placing a
28//! pointing on the image (`SDGrid::getXYPos`), and writing the products are
29//! the task's (#938).
30//!
31//! The gridder holds its two grids whole, as CASA does, under an admission
32//! of [`TotalPowerGrid::bytes`], and grids the image's channels in parallel
33//! on the imaging runtime's worker team.

47/// Why single-dish gridding refused.
48#[derive(Debug, thiserror::Error)]
49pub enum SingleDishError {
50    /// A kernel CASA does not have, or parameters that give no kernel.
51    #[error("{0}")]
52    Kernel(String),
53    /// Weights of a number of correlations the gridder does not image.
54    #[error("{0}")]
55    Correlations(String),
56    /// Arrays that disagree in shape, or an image too large.
57    #[error("{0}")]
58    Shape(String),
