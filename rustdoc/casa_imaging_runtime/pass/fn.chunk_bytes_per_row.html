pub const fn chunk_bytes_per_row(

Expand description

An upper bound of the bytes a pass’s row chunks hold per row of the source’s block, for rows of layout that each produce at most row samples on workers workers, while a chunk’s rows are a block’s share rather than its placement cap: each owner’s buffer and the weight owner’s can take every placement of a row with its native endpoints, and the chunk’s prediction batch every degridded sample and the row’s native terms. The source sizes its blocks with it (crate::finalize_source_access); WaveDemand::bytes charges the chunks exactly.
