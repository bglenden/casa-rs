Expand description

Plan-bound streaming consumer for one validated private prepared artifact.

Chunks are delivered in canonical segment order and never exceed the store’s configured streaming-buffer ceiling. Implementations may retain a bounded decoded cell, but receive no cache path or persistence authority.
