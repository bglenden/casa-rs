The simulator session: CASA’s sm tool in Rust.

A Simulator holds what the sm setters describe, as CASA’s Simulator and NewMSSimulator would have written it to the subtables, and the scans observe plans. It writes nothing until Simulator::close, which creates the MeasurementSet in one streamed pass (plan §4, deferred single-pass execution), so no reader can see an intermediate state. The steps that change DATA (predict, corrupt) are recorded in a plan of which scans each covers, and applied as close writes each row (plan, prediction, corruption); predict prepares its model’s grids when it is called.

A session Simulator::open_from_ms opens on an existing MeasurementSet (sm.openfromms) reads its state back, takes its rows as scans already observed, and patches their DATA in place at close (from_ms).

The session runs within its SessionResources: predict admits its model’s grids, and close what it holds, against the imaging runtime’s resource policy, and close computes each write batch’s geometry, prediction and noise on the runtime’s worker team, so the MeasurementSet is bitwise the same for any budget or worker count. A model is predicted on the CPU or the host’s Metal device (SessionResources), whose predictions agree with the CPU’s to 1e-4 of the largest; noise is drawn on the CPU on either.

The setters keep CASA’s semantics, including order-dependent ones: setfield records the reference time and first spectral window in force when it is called, setconfig and setfeed append, and observe reads the limits, autocorrelation weight and antennas defined at that moment. Where CASA crashes, writes garbage or corrupts data, the session refuses or diverges, as recorded in docs/CASA (C++) bugs.md.
