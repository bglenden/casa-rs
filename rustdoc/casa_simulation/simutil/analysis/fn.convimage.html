simutil.convimage(modelflat, outflat): regrid the flat sky model onto the flat image’s coordinates and shape, and convolve it with the image’s restoring beam.

As CASA does:

  • the model’s flat image is given the image’s restoring beam, in place;
  • <model>.regrid is the model regridded linearly (ia.regrid’s default), scaled by the pixel area as casacore scales an image in Jy/pixel, and labelled Jy/pixel;
  • <model>.regrid.conv is that convolved with the beam (ia.convolve2d), labelled Jy/beam with the image’s beam, and masked where the regrid is.

CASA also computes a second pixel-area factor and multiplies the regrid by it, but takes both of its cells from the image, so the factor is 1. casa-rs leaves it out.

§Errors

An image without one restoring beam, or whose direction coordinate is rotated (CASA’s refusal); a model the regrid refuses; a failure to read or write an image.
