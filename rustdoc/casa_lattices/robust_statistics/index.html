Robust sample statistics: CASA’s statistics-framework algorithms.

C++ casacore implements imstat’s hinges-fences, chauvenet, fit-half and biweight algorithms in casacore/scimath/StatsFramework. This module follows those sources on a materialized sample of (value, linear position) pairs: every algorithm selects its own effective sample from the caller’s, and the statistics reported are the classical ones over that sample.

The quantile rule is the framework’s: the quantile at fraction q of n sorted values is the value at index ceil(q * n) - 1, without interpolation; the median and the median absolute deviation average the two middle values of even samples. The biweight’s initial scale is casacore’s C::probit_3_4 constant, 1.482602218505602 — despite its name, the reciprocal of the probit at 3/4, the factor that makes the median absolute deviation estimate a Gaussian sigma.
