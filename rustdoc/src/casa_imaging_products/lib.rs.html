4//! Native continuum product algorithms and the Product Generation Authority.
5//!
6//! This owner turns one authoritative Major-Cycle result into complete,
7//! generation-consistent continuum products: restoring-beam fitting,
8//! restoration, residual scaling, normalization, validity, metadata, and the
9//! exact member set declared by the compiler-owned Product Graph. It owns the
10//! entire generation-construction capability behind one two-phase authority:
11//!
12//! 1. [`ProductGenerationAuthority::plan`] binds the exact Product Graph and
13//!    closed typed source commitments into one schema-versioned planned
14//!    generation; and
15//! 2. [`ProductGenerationAuthority::authorize`] seals the produced artifacts
16//!    only against the matching closed typed completions.
17//!
18//! There is no other construction path: planned generations, artifact
19//! identities, and seals have no public constructors and cannot be
20//! reconstructed from digest bytes. Publication consumes only the exact
21//! sealed member set through private prepare → independently atomic member
22//! replacement → per-member terminal receipt choreography.
