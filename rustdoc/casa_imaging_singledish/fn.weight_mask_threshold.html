pub fn weight_mask_threshold(weights: &[f32], minweight: f64) -> Option<f64>
Expand description

do_weight_mask: the weight a pixel must exceed to stay unmasked, minweight times the median of the positive weights through every plane (ia.statistics of the weight image where it is positive), or None when no weight is positive (CASA then sets no mask). The median of an even count is the mean of the middle two.

One departure (docs/CASA (C++) bugs.md): CASA writes the threshold into its mask expression with %f, six decimal places, so a threshold under 5e-7 masks nothing. This is the threshold itself.
