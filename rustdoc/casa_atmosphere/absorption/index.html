The atmosphere’s absorption and phase over a spectral grid (ATM’s RefractiveIndexProfile).

For every channel and layer, the layer’s refractivity per unit path N = N′ + iN″ comes from crate::refractivity at the layer’s mean pressure, temperature and water vapour and the gases’ number densities. Integrating over the layers gives, at the zenith,

  • the opacity τ = Σ N″ⱼ Δzⱼ (nepers), and
  • the phase delay φ = Σ N′ⱼ Δzⱼ (radians; reported in degrees) and the path length L = (λ/2π) φ.

ATM separates the dry atmosphere (the O2, O3, CO, N2O, NO2, SO2, HCl and HCN lines and the dry continuum) from the wet one (the H2O lines and the water continuum), and the dispersive delay (the lines) from the non-dispersive one (the continua). The wet terms are computed for the profile’s own water column (AtmosphereProfile::ground_water_column_m) and scale linearly to any other: a wet quantity for column w is the profile’s times w / w₀.

§Two forms

ChannelAbsorption keeps every species ATM keeps (28 complex values a layer) and answers every per-species accessor. ChannelTotals keeps only the dry lines, dry continuum, H2O lines and H2O continuum (4 a layer), which is all that opacities, path lengths and sky brightness need. AbsorptionProfile works over either; the per-species accessors need the full form.

§Building in parallel

Each channel is a pure function of the atmosphere and its frequency (channel_absorption, channel_totals). A caller that wants channels computed on its own threads computes them and passes them to AbsorptionProfile::from_channels; AbsorptionProfile::new computes them serially. The crate starts no threads. AbsorptionProfile::bytes gives the memory a profile keeps, so a caller can admit it first.
