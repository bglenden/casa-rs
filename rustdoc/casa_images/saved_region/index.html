World-coordinate polygon regions saved with an image.

casacore keeps named regions in an image’s regions table keyword (C++ ImageInterface::defineRegion and getRegion, through RegionHandler). This module reads and writes the world-coordinate polygon classes stored there, a single WCPolygon or a WCUnion whose members are all WCPolygons, and turns a region into a pixel mask or into statistics over the pixels it selects.

As in casacore’s WCRegion::toLCRegion, a polygon drawn on two pixel axes covers the full extent of every other image axis. Its vertices are converted to pixel coordinates plane by plane, at the centre of the polygon axes and the plane’s position on the others. A pixel belongs to a polygon when its centre lies inside it or on its boundary.

Other casacore region classes (WCBox, WCEllipsoid, the LC* pixel regions and compounds other than a union of polygons) are reported by class name and not converted. Removing a saved region is PagedImage::remove_region.

§Example

use casa_coordinates::{CoordinateSystem, DirectionCoordinate, Projection, ProjectionType};
use casa_images::{PagedImage, PolygonRegion};
use casa_types::measures::direction::DirectionRef;

let mut coords = CoordinateSystem::new();
coords.add_coordinate(DirectionCoordinate::new(
    DirectionRef::J2000,
    Projection::new(ProjectionType::SIN),
    [0.0, 0.5],
    [-1e-4, 1e-4],
    [2.0, 2.0],
));
let dir = tempfile::tempdir().unwrap();
let mut image = PagedImage::<f32>::create(vec![5, 5], coords.clone(), dir.path().join("a.image")).unwrap();

let mut region = PolygonRegion::new(&coords, [0, 1]).unwrap();
let corners = [[1.0, 1.0], [3.0, 1.0], [3.0, 3.0], [1.0, 3.0]];
region
    .add_polygon(corners.iter().map(|&[x, y]| {
        let world = coords.to_world(&[x, y]).unwrap();
        [world[0], world[1]]
    }).collect())
    .unwrap();
image.put_polygon_region("box", &region).unwrap();
image.save().unwrap();

let mask = region.mask(&coords, image.shape()).unwrap();
assert_eq!(mask.iter().filter(|&&inside| inside).count(), 9);
assert_eq!(image.get_polygon_region("box").unwrap().polygons.len(), 1);
