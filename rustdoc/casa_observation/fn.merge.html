Merge catalogues (at least one) as CASA’s concat merges their MeasurementSets, within tolerance.

§Errors

ObservationError::Unsupported for what CASA’s predicates cannot match or this merge does not: antenna positions in different frames (casacore refuses them) and a window whose channels match another’s only reversed. (A catalogue holds no field in a solar-system frame, which CASA matches by frame alone: no store’s adapter represents one.)
