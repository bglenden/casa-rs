Expand description

What one partition’s traversal will cost, computed from the catalogue and the store’s geometry before any payload is read.

Fields§

§partition: PartitionId

The partition.

§times: usize

Times the traversal delivers: the grid’s, or those the request’s time and field selections hold.

§time_runs: TimeRuns

Which they are, as indices into the grid’s times.

§weights: Option<WeightSource>

Which rule and variable supply the delivered weights, when the weights role is read.

§baselines: usize

Baselines on its grid.

§bytes_per_sample: usize

Bytes one sample of the requested roles holds in a batch (Roles::sample_bytes).

§bytes_per_time: usize

Bytes one time holds in a batch besides its samples’ (Roles::time_bytes).

§scratch_bytes_per_sample: usize

Bytes per sample the cursor’s own scratch holds while it fills a batch: what it decodes before gathering a correlation subset, converting flags or placing rows around padding.

§times_per_batch: usize

Whole times per full batch under the budget.

§batches: usize

Batches in the traversal.

§decode_units: usize

Physical read units (casacore rows, Zarr chunks) the traversal touches, as modelled from the store’s layout: the chunks that intersect each batch’s selection. A model, not a measurement: the cursor’s IoCounters::decode_units applies the same model to what it reads.

§decoded_elements: usize

Elements the touched units hold, over every role read, by the same model.

§selected_elements: usize

Elements the request selects, over every role read; the ratio to decoded_elements is the read amplification.

§natural_blocks: NaturalBlocks

The store’s natural blocks along time and frequency.

§padded_fraction: f64

The fraction of grid cells the adapter pads because no acquisition fills them: what an MSv2 adapter places rows around. An MSv4 store holds its grid whole, so its adapter pads nothing and reports 0; that says nothing of cells the store itself holds as padding (XRADIO’s NaN cells), which the delivered flags mark.
