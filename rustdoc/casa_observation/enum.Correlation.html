Expand description

The type of one entry on a visibility’s polarization axis.

These are casacore’s Stokes::StokesTypes, every defined value but Undefined, and the same 32 values the MSv4 schema allows in its polarization coordinate. The persistent forms are the casacore code, stored by MeasurementSet v2 in POLARIZATION::CORR_TYPE, and the label ("XX", "RCircular") MSv4 stores. Variant names are the labels.

Most stores hold receptor products (XX, RL); a store may also hold Stokes parameters, single-dish polarizations or derived quantities, and a consumer refuses the types it cannot use.
