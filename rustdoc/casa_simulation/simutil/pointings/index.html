Module pointings

Mosaic pointings: simutil.calc_pointings2 and the pointing files simobserve reads and writes (<project>.ptg.txt).

A pointing is a direction string as simutil writes them: J2000 18:59:57.29543 -023.00.38.805516.

PointingFile
A pointing file’s pointings and their times in seconds (0 where a line gives none).

Enums§

MapType
The layouts calc_pointings2 knows, by the first three characters of maptype in upper case (hex, hexagonal, ALMA-OT, alma, …).

Constants§

MOST_POINTINGS
The most pointings calc_pointings lays out. CASA lays out a map of any size, and with a spacing tiny beside the map runs for hours or out of memory; casa-rs refuses such a map before laying any of it out.
PBCOEFF
simobserve’s pbcoeff: the primary beam is 1.13 λ/D. calc_pointings2’s ALMA tiling uses it for its default beam, where CASA reads an undefined name.

Functions§

calc_pointings
simutil.calc_pointings2(spacing, size, maptype, direction, relmargin, beam): the pointings of a map size (x, y) with pointings spacing apart about direction, starting at the top row. relmargin is the margin in spacings left at each edge (hexagonal and square maps); beam_arcsec is the primary beam the ALMA tiling fills to (spacing × 1.13 × √3 when not positive).
format_pointings
simutil.write_pointings(filename, pointings, time)’s text: a header, then each pointing, two spaces and its time. times are written as given (Python’s str of each); one time is used for every pointing.
parse_pointings
simutil.read_pointings of a file’s text. Lines starting #, RA or -- are skipped, as are lines with fewer than three fields. A line is either epoch ra dec [time] or an Observing Tool line, ra, dec, SEXAGESIMAL, ..., time, whose direction is taken as ICRS (and whose declination, given with colons, is read as hours and divided by 15). As in CASA, reading stops silently at the first line that fails; a file with no pointings is refused.
read_pointings
simutil.read_pointings(filename).
triangle_points
simutil.getTrianglePoints: the Cycle 0 tiling, offsets in arcsec.
triangular_tiling
simutil.getTriangularTiling: the Observing Tool’s tiling (Cycle 1 on), laid along the map’s longer side, offsets in arcsec. An odd number of rows across the short side takes the base tiling, an even number the shifted one.
write_pointings
format_pointings written to path, each time a float as Python writes it.
